Who We Are

Bringing Digital Governance to Badnapur

Established in 2018, Khan MultiServices & CSC Center was founded with a singular vision: to bridge the digital gap in our region. Located near the Nasib Tea House on Main Road, Badnapur, Maharashtra, we have become the go-to resource for citizens seeking convenient and reliable government, banking, and digital services.

Instead of travelling hours to district offices in Jalna, local residents can now process vital credentials like Aadhaar corrections, PAN card updates, caste certificates, income declarations, and digital bank withdrawals right here in their neighborhood, saving valuable time and cost.

Our Mission

To empower citizens by providing secure, quick, and transparent access to essential services and government welfare schemes.

Our Vision

To remain the region's most trusted digital facilitator, known for integrity, accurate handling, and excellent customer care.
